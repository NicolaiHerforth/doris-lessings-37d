# Danish Forums & Community Sites

## 1. Boligdebatten.dk
- **URL:** https://www.boligdebatten.dk
- **What:** Danish housing forum (powered by vBulletin). Associated with Boliga.dk.
- **How:** Register and post in relevant section. Active community discussing buying/selling.
- **Post:**

```
Emne: Rækkehus til salg i Ørestad Syd — 150m², tagterrasse, 2017

Hej alle,

Vi har sat vores rækkehus på Doris Lessings Vej 37D i Ørestad Syd til salg og vil gerne dele det her.

- 150m² split-level rækkehus
- 5 værelser
- 12m² tagterrasse
- Bygget 2017, energimærke A2020
- Udbudspris: 8.250.000 kr

Vi har lavet en side med alle detaljer og billeder: dorislessingsvej37d.com

Mægler er Lasse Sundberg hos LokalBolig.

Er der nogen her med erfaring med at sælge rækkehuse i Ørestad? Vil gerne høre hvad markedet er pt.
```

## 2. DBA.dk (Den Blå Avis)
- **URL:** https://www.dba.dk
- **What:** Denmark's largest classifieds site. Has a "Bolig til salg" section.
- **How:** Create a listing under Bolig → Huse til salg
- **Note:** This is a MAJOR channel. Many Danes browse DBA for housing. Free or very cheap to list.
- **Priority:** 🔴 HIGH — do this on Day 1

---

## 3. Ørestad Community / Grundejerforening
- **Grundejerforeningen Ørestad Syd** — the local homeowners' association
- Check if they have a newsletter, noticeboard, or internal forum
- **Action:** Ask the grundejerforening if they can include it in any neighborhood communication
- **Facebook group:** facebook.com/groups/oerestad/ (Ørestad community group)

---

## 4. Ørestad Innovation City Copenhagen (OICC)
- **URL:** https://oicc.dk
- **What:** Ørestad's community organization
- **Mentions:** Ørestad Social Club and other community groups
- **Action:** Check if OICC has community boards or newsletter where housing can be shared

---

## 5. GulogGratis.dk
- **URL:** https://www.guloggratis.dk
- **What:** Danish classifieds (alternative to DBA)
- **How:** Post under Bolig → Til salg
- **Priority:** 🟡 MED

---

## 6. Selvsalg.dk
- **URL:** https://www.selvsalg.dk
- **What:** Self-sale property platform (linked to Boliga/Boligdebatten)
- **Note:** Since you have a mægler, this isn't the right platform. Skip.

---

## 7. Boligsiden.dk
- **URL:** https://www.boligsiden.dk
- **What:** Major Danish property portal
- **Note:** Your mægler should already have this listed. Verify it's there.
