## 2. Price Strategy

### The core problem

Your udbudspris (8,250,000) is approximately **10-15% above realistic market value**. In a functional market, this means:
- Buyers who can afford 8.5M are comparing you to better alternatives
- Buyers whose budget is 7-7.5M don't even look because you're filtered out
- Your listing becomes "stale" on Boligsiden the longer it sits

### Standard Danish approaches

**A. Prisnedsættelse (price reduction)**
The most common and effective lever. In Denmark:
- Average prisnedslag nationally: ~5-7% from first udbudspris to final sale price (Finans Danmark data)
- For houses: average afslag of ~158,000 kr on a 2.4M house (~6.5%) in late 2024
- First reduction should be **meaningful enough to reach a new buyer segment**

**A 200,000 kr drop (to 8,295,000) is pointless.** It doesn't change who sees the listing or signals desperation.

**Recommended options:**
1. **Drop to 7,995,000** — breaks the 8M psychological barrier. Buyers searching "under 8M" now see you. Still ~500K above comps, leaving negotiation room.
2. **Drop to 7,495,000** — aggressive but realistic. Signals you're serious. Likely to generate offers quickly. Final sale around 7.2-7.4M after negotiation.
3. **Drop to 7,750,000** — middle ground. Below 8M barrier, closer to market reality.

**B. "Åben for bud" (open for offers)**
- Removes the listed price entirely — signals flexibility
- Can work if you want to test the market without committing to a number
- Risk: buyers lowball aggressively; you may get offers at 6.5-7M
- Better suited for properties that have been on market 100+ days
- **Not recommended yet at 38 days**

**C. Strategic timing of reduction**
- The *best* time to reduce is just before peak season: **late February / early March**
- A price reduction on Boligsiden triggers a notification to everyone who has saved a search matching your criteria — this is essentially free re-marketing
- Do NOT do multiple small reductions (looks desperate). One significant drop is better.

### Psychological price points in Denmark

Danish buyers search in brackets. Key thresholds:
- **Under 8,000,000** — massive filter. Your listing at 8,250,000 is invisible to this entire segment.
- **Under 7,500,000** — another major bracket
- The difference between 8,250,000 and 7,995,000 isn't just 500K — it's visibility to an entirely different buyer pool

### What the math says about your situation

You bought for 6,200,000 in 2021. Even at 7,500,000 you'd clear:
- Gross gain: 1,300,000 kr
- Minus mægler (1%): ~75,000
- Minus improvement costs (if any)
- Still a solid return after 4-5 years

At 8,250,000 you'd make 2,295,000 — but only if someone pays it. **A property that doesn't sell makes zero.**

## 3. Mægler Accountability

### What Lasse should be doing after 30+ days

A competent Danish mægler should proactively:
1. **Contact you for a status meeting** after 2-4 weeks with data
2. **Provide performance metrics** including:
   - Number of Boligsiden views/clicks on your listing
   - Number of enquiries received
   - Number of showings conducted (private + åbent hus)
   - Buyer feedback from every showing
   - Comparable sales activity in the area
3. **Propose a revised strategy** — not just "let's wait and see"
4. **Actively contact potential buyers** from their database

### KPIs you can and should ask for

| Metric | What it tells you |
|--------|-------------------|
| Boligsiden visninger (views) | Is the listing being seen? |
| Boligsiden klik (clicks to full listing) | Are people interested enough to click? |
| Antal henvendelser | How many actual buyer contacts? |
| Antal fremvisninger | How many showings? |
| Feedback fra fremvisninger | What's turning buyers away? (Price? Layout? Area?) |
| Sammenligning med lignende | How does your traffic compare to similar listings? |

### What the formidlingsaftale typically requires

Per Danish law (Lov om formidling af fast ejendom):
- The agreement must specify: udbudspris, salær, other costs, and termination conditions
- Standard duration: **6 months** (with "solgt eller gratis" + opsigelsesgebyr clauses)
- Can be extended 3 months at a time
- **Can always be terminated without notice** — but mægleren may have a claim to a "rimeligt vederlag" (reasonable fee) if YOU terminate and there's a result-dependent fee clause
- Review your specific agreement for what's owed upon early termination

### What LokalBolig specifically should provide

- Boligsiden analytics dashboard (they have access)
- Comparison data: how many days similar listings in 2300 have been on market
- Their honest assessment: is price the issue, or is it marketing?
- A concrete action plan with timeline

## 5. Seller's Own Levers

### Staging adjustments
- **Depersonalize**: Remove family photos, quirky decor. Buyers need to project themselves.
- **Declutter aggressively**: 50% less stuff than you think. Especially in 150 m² where room sizes matter.
- **Light**: February is dark in Denmark. Every lamp on, bright bulbs, curtains fully open.
- **Smell**: Fresh coffee, no cooking smells, no pets. Basic but effective.
- **Fix the small stuff**: Scuffed walls, dripping taps, squeaky doors. Buyers notice everything at 8M.

### Open house timing
- **Best days in Denmark**: Sunday (most common), Saturday afternoon
- **Best time**: 11:00-13:00 (Sunday) or 13:00-15:00 (Saturday)
- **Worst time**: Weekday evenings (low turnout), Friday
- **February considerations**: Daylight matters. 11-13 maximizes natural light.
- Tomorrow's open house (Feb 15 = Sunday) is actually well-timed

### Strategic price reduction timing
- **Best**: Reduce price in late February, just before the spring market (March-May)
- **The Boligsiden notification effect**: When you reduce, everyone with matching saved searches gets notified. This is a powerful free marketing tool. Time it strategically.
- **Don't reduce the day before an open house** — do it Monday/Tuesday to capture the week's search traffic

### Creating urgency
- **Deadline for offers ("tilbudsfrist")**: Less common in DK than some markets, but legal. "Bud modtages inden [date]"
- **Two-buyer dynamics**: If you get interest from multiple parties after a price drop, let them know (truthfully) that others are interested
- **Spring leverage**: "We're pricing to sell before March" signals motivation without desperation

## 6. When to Consider Switching Mægler

### Danish rules
- You can **always** terminate the formidlingsaftale
- No mandatory notice period (unless your specific contract says otherwise)
- **BUT**: If you have a "solgt eller gratis" agreement with an opsigelsesgebyr, you may owe a fee upon termination. Check your contract.
- Typical opsigelsesgebyr: covers mægler's documented costs (photos, marketing materials, etc.) — usually 10,000-30,000 kr
- After termination, there may be a **pipeline clause**: if someone who was introduced during the contract period buys within 3-6 months, the original mægler may claim commission

### Is switching at 38 days premature?
**Yes, likely premature.** Here's why:
- 38 days is below Copenhagen average liggetid
- You haven't tried the most obvious lever (price reduction)
- Switching mægler without changing price = same result
- A new mægler needs 2-3 weeks to photograph, list, and market — you lose momentum
- **The issue is almost certainly price, not mægler performance**

### When it WOULD make sense to switch
- After 90+ days with no meaningful interest despite a market-aligned price
- If Lasse cannot provide basic performance data
- If marketing quality is objectively poor (bad photos, weak description)
- If communication has broken down
- If you discover the formidlingsaftale terms are unfavorable

### The real question
**Before switching mægler, ask: "Would a different mægler sell this at 8.5M?"** The answer is almost certainly no. The next mægler will tell you the same thing: the price needs to come down.

## 7. What to Say to Lasse — Conversation Script

### Setup
Request a dedicated meeting (not just a quick call). In person or video. Frame it as collaborative.

### Opening

> "Lasse, tak fordi du tager dig tid. Vi er nu 38 dage inde, og jeg vil gerne have en ærlig status på, hvor vi står, og hvad planen er fremadrettet."

### Phase 1: Get the data

> "Kan du dele de konkrete tal med mig?
> - Hvor mange visninger har vi haft på Boligsiden?
> - Hvor mange har klikket ind på den fulde annonce?
> - Hvor mange henvendelser har du fået?
> - Hvad har feedback været fra dem, der har set boligen — hvad er det, der holder dem tilbage?"

*If he doesn't have this data, that's a red flag. If he does, listen carefully.*

### Phase 2: Address the elephant — price

> "Jeg har kigget på de seneste handler på Doris Lessings Vej og i Ørestad generelt. De 117 m² rækkehuse på vejen sælges til omkring 5,9-6,1 mio. Selv de større enheder lander på 48-52.000 kr/m². Vores udbudspris svarer til knap 57.000 kr/m² — det er i den høje ende.
>
> Hvad er din ærlige vurdering: er vi prisat rigtigt, eller skal vi justere?"

*Let him respond. If he agrees the price is high, move to Phase 3. If he defends 8.5M, ask:*

> "Hvilke handler i området understøtter vores pris? Kan du vise mig sammenlignelige salg af 150 m² rækkehuse over 8 mio i Ørestad?"

### Phase 3: Discuss a specific plan

> "Jeg er åben for en prisjustering, men jeg vil gerne gøre det strategisk. Hvad tænker du om at gå ned til 7.995.000 — under 8 mio-grænsen — i slutningen af februar, lige inden forårsmarkedet?
>
> Og i den forbindelse: hvad gør vi marketingmæssigt for at maksimere effekten? Har vi brugt Boligsiden Boost? Kører vi noget på sociale medier? Er boligen delt på tværs af LokalBolig-kontorer?"

### Phase 4: Set expectations and timeline

> "Lad os aftale en konkret plan:
> 1. Hvad gør vi de næste to uger?
> 2. Hvornår evaluerer vi igen?
> 3. Hvad er success-kriterierne — hvor mange henvendelser/fremvisninger bør vi forvente efter en prisændring?
>
> Jeg vil gerne have en skriftlig opfølgning med de aftalte tiltag og en tidsplan."

### Phase 5: Accountability without aggression

> "Jeg er tilfreds med samarbejdet, og jeg tror vi kan lande det her. Men jeg har brug for at vide, at der bliver gjort alt, hvad der kan gøres. Kan vi aftale ugentlige status-opdateringer med de nøgletal, vi talte om?"

### If he pushes back or is vague

> "Lasse, jeg forstår det er et svært marked, og jeg forventer ikke magi. Men jeg forventer data, en klar plan, og proaktiv kommunikation. Hvis vi ikke kan blive enige om en vej frem, er jeg nødt til at overveje mine muligheder."
