# Google Ads Campaign Plan

## Budget: 500 kr (~€67)
## Duration: 14 days
## Daily budget: ~36 kr/day

---

## Campaign Structure

**Goal:** Capture people actively searching for housing in Ørestad/Copenhagen S

#### Ad Group 1: Ørestad Housing
**Keywords:**
- rækkehus ørestad
- bolig til salg ørestad

- doris lessings vej

## Targeting Settings
- **Location:** Copenhagen metropolitan area (radius 30 km)
- **Language:** Danish (Campaign 1), English (Campaign 2)
- **Bid strategy:** Maximize clicks (for a short campaign with limited budget)
- **Device:** All devices (mobile-first — most property searches are mobile)
- **Schedule:** All day (property searches happen evenings + weekends too)

## Negative Keywords
- leje / rent / udlejning
- kollegium / dorm
- andelsbolig / andel
- free / gratis
- cheap / billig

## Expected Performance
- **CPC estimate:** 3-8 kr for Danish, 5-12 kr for English keywords
- **Estimated clicks:** 50-120 over 14 days
- **Landing page visits:** 40-100 (accounting for bounce)
