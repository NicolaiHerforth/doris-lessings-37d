# Expat Forums & Sites

## 1. Expat.com — Copenhagen Forum
- **URL:** https://www.expat.com/en/forum/europe/denmark/copenhagen/
- **What:** Active expat forum with housing discussions
- **How:** Create a forum post in the Copenhagen section. Frame as "townhouse for sale in Ørestad" — the forum allows housing posts.
- **Post:**

```
Subject: Modern townhouse for sale in Ørestad, Copenhagen — 150m², roof terrace

Hello Copenhagen expats!

We're selling our townhouse in Ørestad Syd. If you or anyone you know is looking to buy in Copenhagen, this might be interesting.

- 150m² split-level townhouse, 5 rooms
- Private 12m² roof terrace
- Built 2017, energy rating A2020
- 2 min to Vestamager Metro, 12 min to city center
- Asking: 8,250,000 DKK

Ørestad is a fantastic area for expats — modern, international community, close to everything.

Full details and photos: https://dorislessingsvej37d.com/index-en.html

Contact our agent (LokalBolig) through the listing for viewings. Thanks!
```

## 2. InterNations — Copenhagen Expat Forum
- **URL:** https://www.internations.org/copenhagen-expats/forum/46
- **What:** Premium expat network with forums and events
- **How:** Requires free membership. Post in the Copenhagen forum under housing/accommodation topic.
- **Note:** InterNations is quite active for Copenhagen expats. Many members are professionals who can afford to buy.
- **Use same template as Expat.com** (above), adjusted for tone.

---

## 3. The Local Denmark
- **URL:** https://www.thelocal.dk
- **What:** English-language news site for Denmark. No classifieds section, but has a membership community.
- **How:** Limited options for direct posting. Better as a reference/backlink source.
- **Alternative:** Check if they have a community section or partner with housing ads.

---

## 4. International House Copenhagen
- **URL:** https://ihcph.kk.dk
- **What:** Official Copenhagen municipality resource for internationals
- **How:** They have bulletin boards (physical and sometimes digital) for the international community.
- **Action:** Check if they have a digital noticeboard or community forum. If physical only, print a flyer.

---

## 5. Denmark for Expats
- **URL:** https://www.denmarkforexpats.com
- **What:** Resource site with marketplace section
- **How:** Check their marketplace for housing listings. Post if available.

---

## 6. EasyExpat.com — Copenhagen Classifieds
- **URL:** https://www.easyexpat.com/en/classifieds/copenhagen/accommodation/
- **What:** Expat classifieds site with housing section
- **How:** Post a free classified ad in the Copenhagen accommodation section.
- **Use Expat.com template** (above).
