## Tips
- Post the Danish version first (most of your network is probably Danish)
- Post the English version 2-3 days later OR as a comment on the Danish post
- Add 3-5 best photos as a carousel (roof terrace, living room, exterior)
- Lead photo: roof terrace or the most eye-catching interior shot
- Ask close colleagues to like/comment early to boost algorithm reach
- Pin as "Featured" on your profile for extra visibility
