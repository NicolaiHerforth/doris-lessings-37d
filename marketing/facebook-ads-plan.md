# Facebook & Instagram Ads Campaign Plan

## Budget: 1,500 kr (~€200)
## Duration: 14 days
## Daily budget: ~107 kr/day

---

## Campaign Structure

### Campaign 1: Danish Families (900 kr / 64 kr/day)
**Objective:** Traffic (drive to landing page)

#### Audience Targeting:
- **Location:** Copenhagen + 20 km radius
- **Age:** 28-45
- **Interests:**
  - Bolig / Ejendomme / Real estate
  - Boligsiden, Boliga, LokalBolig
  - Home & Garden
  - Family & Parenting
- **Behaviors:**
  - Likely to move (Facebook predicts this!)
  - Homeowners OR renters (both — renters may be upgrading)
- **Exclude:** Under 25, interests in student housing

#### Placements:
- Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Instagram Stories, Facebook Marketplace

#### Ad Creative:

**Ad 1 — Carousel (best performer for real estate)**
- Card 1: Exterior/street view → "150m² rækkehus i Ørestad"

## Image Recommendations
Best photos to use (in priority order):
1. **Roof terrace** — the money shot, summer vibes
2. **Living room** — show the light and split-level design
3. **Exterior** — the full townhouse from street level
4. **Kitchen** — modern, clean
5. **Master bedroom** — spacious feel
6. **Neighborhood** — Ørestad with metro, nature, modern architecture

**Format:** 1080x1080 (square) for feed, 1080x1920 (vertical) for Stories
**Text on images:** Keep under 20% text (Facebook penalizes text-heavy images)

---

## Optimization Schedule
- **Day 1-3:** Run all ads, gather data
- **Day 4:** Pause underperforming ads (CTR < 0.8%)
- **Day 7:** Reallocate budget to best-performing ad/audience
- **Day 10:** Refresh creative if fatigue sets in (CTR dropping)
- **Day 14:** Campaign ends

## Expected Performance
- **CPM:** 40-80 kr (Copenhagen real estate audience)
- **CTR:** 1-2%
- **CPC:** 3-7 kr
- **Estimated clicks:** 200-500
- **Landing page visits:** 150-400
