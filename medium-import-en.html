Why Ørestad is Copenhagen's Best-Kept Secret for Young Families

When people think of Copenhagen neighbourhoods, they think Nørrebro, Vesterbro, Frederiksberg. Maybe Østerbro if they have kids. Almost nobody mentions Ørestad. That's exactly why it works.

The neighbourhood nobody expected to love

Ørestad Syd used to be a punchline — a windswept construction site south of the city. That was five years ago. Today it's something else entirely. The canals catch the morning light. Amager Fælled — thousands of hectares of open nature — starts at your back door. The metro takes you to Kongens Nytorv in twelve minutes. You can cycle to the beach in ten.

What makes it work for families is the combination of space and access. Try finding a 150 m² home in Copenhagen with a private rooftop terrace, a garden courtyard, and a playground outside your door — for under €1.2 million. In Østerbro, that's a fantasy. In Ørestad, it exists.

A different kind of home

The Bella row is a cluster of five townhouses built in 2017, tucked away from the main road in their own little pocket of calm. Each home has six split levels — half-storeys that give every zone its own purpose and its own feeling. Kitchen and dining with doors opening onto the terrace. Half a level up: the living room with floor-to-ceiling windows. Another half: the home office. And at the very top: your private rooftop terrace with sunset views over the city.

It's the kind of architecture that makes you forget you're in an apartment-dominated city. This is a house. A proper house, with three floors of living space and a Gaggenau kitchen.

What the numbers say

150 m² living space. Five rooms. Energy rating A2020 — monthly costs are remarkably low. Built 2017, so everything is modern and well-maintained. Two minutes' walk to Vestamager metro station. Five minutes to Fields shopping centre. Twelve minutes to the city centre by metro.

For expats: Denmark has some of the most favourable mortgage terms in Europe. Fixed-rate loans at historically low rates, with tax-deductible interest payments. The monthly cost of owning this home may surprise you.
